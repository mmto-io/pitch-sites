A world of flavour, a village at heart +

Our menu offers a selection of imaginative pizzas, with flavour profiles inspired by global cuisines and local West Coast ingredients. Our seafood options pay homage to Steveston’s storied fishing past, while other creations reflect the flavours of the diverse cultures that shape modern Richmond. From Asian-inspired ingredients to Mediterranean garnishes, we believe in telling stories through food.

Whether you’re a vegetarian, a seafood enthusiast, or an adventurous eater, there’s something to discover. Not sure where to start? Try one of our signature creations — crafted to surprise and satisfy.
