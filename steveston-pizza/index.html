04 / THE HANDSONE CHEF. EVERY SINGLE PIZZA.

Not just made.
Handcrafted.

We believe great pizza starts with great ingredients. That’s why we use organic flour, house-made sauces, and thoughtfully sourced toppings to create pizzas that are as nourishing as they are delicious. From slow-roasted vegetables and artisan meats to carefully selected seafood, every detail is crafted with intention.

Our owner and Head Chef, Nader, personally handcrafts every single pizza that leaves our shop to ensure quality control. People are important to us, and our staff pride themselves on getting to know our customers by name — creating a warm, welcoming experience that goes beyond just great food.

We take inspiration from Steveston’s coastal heritage, a community long known for its fishing roots and culinary traditions. This deep connection to the water inspires many of our seafood-driven creations. Just as importantly, we embrace Richmond’s rich cultural diversity — drawing flavour, technique, and creativity from the multicultural community around us. The result is pizza that’s elevated, unexpected, and full of local character.
